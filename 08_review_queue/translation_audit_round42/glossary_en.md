**修改前**

```markdown
**Gnosticism**: An umbrella term for a cluster of ancient religious traditions and philosophical movements. ```
```

**修改后**

```markdown
**Gnosticism**: An umbrella term for a group of ancient religious ideas and traditions. [Human Interior and Classifications](https://iep.utm.edu/gnostic/)
```

**修改后**

